The Pulse · Story of the day

Psychotherapies outperform other treatments for PTSD-related sleep issues

A systematic review of 27 studies shows cognitive-behavioural therapy significantly improves sleep in PTSD patients compared to relaxation techniques or no treatment. Meta-analysis reveals a 42% greater likelihood of sleep improvement. Useful when refining treatment protocols for PTSD-related insomnia.

REM onset markers in narcolepsy show poor reliability across tests

In 227 untreated narcolepsy type 1 patients, nocturnal sleep-onset REM periods (nSOREMPs) and direct REM (nDREM) transitions showed low test-retest stability. Cross-sectional, so causality is unproven. Discuss at your next diagnostic review to refine criteria.

New criteria improve nocturnal hypoventilation detection in ALS

In a study of 142 ALS patients, new diagnostic criteria identified nocturnal hypoventilation with 79% sensitivity and 85% specificity, up from 65% and 70% under previous criteria. Useful when updating protocols for ALS sleep management.
