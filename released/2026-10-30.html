The Pulse · Story of the day

Permanent DST delays school sunrise commutes for over a billion children

The US House's approval of permanent daylight saving time (DST) would delay sunrise school commutes for over a billion K-12 children. Health, sleep, and scientific groups favour standard time (ST) to align solar, biological, and social rhythms, especially for adolescents. This shift impacts children's sleep and health. Trust flag: summary lacks specific data on impacts.

Routine sleep measures often miss true sleep duration in EEG

Systematic review links RLS to higher Parkinson's risk

A systematic-narrative review and meta-analysis of 21 studies found restless legs syndrome (RLS) is associated with a 2.5 times higher risk of Parkinson's disease. Useful when refining risk stratification for neurological disorders in your clinical practice.

Media use linked to poorer sleep in children, but effects vary

An umbrella review of media use and sleep in children found that screen time correlates with reduced sleep duration and quality, but effects vary by device and content type. Cross-sectional. Useful when updating sleep guidelines for paediatric patients, discuss at your next sleep disorder review.

New Ru-SATED 4.0 scale validated for assessing six sleep health dimensions

The Ru-SATED 4.0 scale, validated in a representative U.S. sample of 2,000 adults, evaluates six sleep dimensions: Regularity, Satisfaction, Alertness, Timing, Efficiency, and Duration. Self-reported data. Useful for clinicians when assessing multidimensional sleep health in patients.

Intravascular monitoring tracks deep oxygen changes in pigs

A proof-of-concept study in three pigs used intravascular diffuse reflectance spectroscopy to monitor continuous oxygen saturation in abdominal aorta, central veins, and renal veins. The technique tracked specified oxygen changes, though it remains investigational. Discuss at your next research planning meeting to explore potential applications.
