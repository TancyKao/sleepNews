A national register-based case-control study found that patients with central hypersomnolence disorders had a 21% lower income and incurred 37% higher healthcare costs compared to controls. Cross-sectional data. Useful when refining policy and care strategies for sleep-disordered patients.

Shorter sleep tied to higher gastric disease risk in study

A Chinese prospective cohort study of over 50,000 adults found that sleeping less than 6 hours nightly was linked to a 23% higher risk of new-onset gastric diseases. Cohort study; useful when advising middle-aged and older patients on sleep habits.
