The Pulse · Story of the day

Noncontact radar lamp accurately stages sleep in diverse cohorts

A new noncontact radar system (Sleepal AI Lamp) stages sleep with accuracy matching polysomnography in 1022 adults, including those with obstructive sleep apnea. Frequency-augmented deep learning interprets respiratory and movement signals. Vendor validation. This shifts sleep staging to a more accessible and feasible method, useful for integrating into clinical protocols.

World Sleep Society sets standards to harmonise wearable sleep data

Solriamfetol improves CPAP adherence in OSA patients with residual sleepiness

A French database analysis of 1,200 OSA patients shows Solriamfetol boosts CPAP adherence by 15% among those with persistent daytime sleepiness. Vendor-funded study; useful when reassessing pharmacological support for CPAP compliance.

Insomnia linked to brain network abnormalities across multiple studies

Across 63 studies, 3391 ID patients showed structural and functional abnormalities in networks like ventral attention, sensorimotor, and default mode. Microscale integration provides deeper insights but lacks longitudinal data. Useful for refining diagnostic biomarkers and research protocols.
