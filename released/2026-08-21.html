Machine learning predicts sleepiness improvement after CPAP in OSA patients

A multi-cohort study using baseline polysomnographic metrics predicted ESS improvements post-CPAP treatment. Trained on 139 participants, the model accurately forecasted changes in a holdout group of 74. Useful for clinicians when planning patient-specific CPAP regimens.

A review suggests integrating lifestyle and metabolic factors into obstructive sleep apnoea (OSA) management to better tailor patient support. This cross-sectional study highlights the need for a more personalised approach but lacks longitudinal data. Useful when refining treatment protocols for patients with comorbidities.

Neurons activated by wakefulness found to regulate sleep drive in mice

In mice, whole-brain activity mapping identified neurons that increase with prolonged wakefulness and regulate subsequent sleep drive. This finding advances our understanding of homeostatic sleep regulation but lacks immediate clinical application. Useful when planning future studies on neural circuits controlling sleep.

Sleep patterns vary across menstrual cycle in psychiatric patients

In a study of 142 psychiatric patients, subjective and objective sleep measures fluctuated significantly across the menstrual cycle. Wearable data from 39 participants confirmed these fluctuations. Useful when refining patient support protocols for sleep management based on menstrual phase.
