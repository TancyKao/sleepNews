New CAP scoring rules from ESS taskforce transform sleep EEG analysis

Odour and sound cues together nullify sleep reactivation benefits for memory

In a study, concurrent presentation of memory-related odours and sounds during sleep eliminated the benefits usually seen from targeted memory reactivation. This cross-sectional finding suggests that timing and modality matter in sleep-based learning enhancement techniques. Useful when designing future studies on sleep reactivation protocols.
