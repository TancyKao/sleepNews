Multimodal sleep intervention improved sleep and mental health in Army trainees

A study of 83 Infantry trainees found that a multimodal sleep intervention, including psychoeducation and sleep hygiene optimisation, improved subjective sleep outcomes and reduced internalising symptoms compared to a control group. Self-reported data, no code yet. Discuss how to integrate such interventions in high-stress training environments.

Narcolepsy EEG profile in comorbid OSA resembles narcolepsy more closely

In a study of 50 patients, the EEG spectral profile of narcolepsy with comorbid OSA more closely resembled narcolepsy than OSA alone. Cross-sectional study with limited generalisability. Read to refine diagnostic protocols for sleep disorders with comorbidities.
