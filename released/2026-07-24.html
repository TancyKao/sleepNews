The Pulse · Story of the day

Polysomnographic traits distinguish CSA from OSA in heart failure patients

In a trial of HF patients with reduced LVEF and AHI >15, loop gain and arousal threshold helped discriminate between central (CSA) and obstructive sleep apnea (OSA). Logistic regression showed higher loop gain in CSA. Cross-sectional data; useful for refining diagnostic protocols for HF-related sleep disorders.

Low muscle strength and being underweight raise insomnia risk in older adults

A cross-sectional study of 5824 older adults found that low muscle strength increased the odds of insomnia by 1.22 (95% CI: 1.02–1.46), after adjusting for depressive symptoms. Cross-sectional data; useful when refining patient assessment protocols to include muscle strength and body weight in late-life insomnia evaluations.
