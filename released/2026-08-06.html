The Pulse · Story of the day

Alixorexton reduces daytime sleepiness in narcolepsy and hypersomnia trials

In a Phase 1b trial, Alixorexton targeting orexin 2 receptors cut daytime sleepiness by over 50% in patients with idiopathic hypersomnia or narcolepsy. Randomised proof-of-concept; could refine treatment protocols for these conditions.

New method quantifies cardiopulmonary modulation using ECG and respiration signals

A study in patients with hypertension and ischaemic heart disease uses multilayer visibility graph analysis on 10-minute ECG and respiration recordings. Predefined signal-quality pipelines ensure data integrity before constructing graphs from clean segments. Cross-sectional, so causality remains unproven. Worth benchmarking against existing cardiopulmonary analysis methods in your next study protocol.
