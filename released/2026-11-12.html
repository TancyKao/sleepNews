The Pulse · Story of the day

Slow waves during wakefulness linked to hallucinations in Parkinson's

In 84 nondemented PD patients, sleep-like slow waves during wakefulness predicted hallucinations and poorer cognition. This biomarker may aid in diagnosing and monitoring PD complications. Useful for clinicians assessing cognitive and psychiatric symptoms in PD.

Unreadable ECG data outstrips misclassification in wearables

In three diagnostic databases, 88% of unreadable ECG windows came from one subject, highlighting deployment error over misclassification. Analyzability measured with an unsupervised morphological criterion. Cross-dataset analysis shows sparse unreadability. Vendor-supplied data. Useful when evaluating wearable ECG reliability in your sleep protocol.
